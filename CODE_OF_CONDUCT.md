# GR Taskbar Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

GR Studios AI LLC is committed to maintaining a respectful,
professional, inclusive, and welcoming community.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat developers and community members with respect.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Help maintain a productive development environment.
- Respect intellectual property and software licenses.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Spam, malicious content, or deliberate disruption.
- Abusive behavior toward developers or community members.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove inappropriate content,
restrict participation, or block accounts when necessary.

Report inappropriate behavior privately through an
available GitHub reporting or maintainer contact channel.
